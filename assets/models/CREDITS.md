Converted from the original FBX files with Blender 4.5: textures resized to
1024 px (albedo as JPEG, normal map), one animation per file, the rig's IK
baked into the bones. Attribution isn't required by CC0, but it's given
anyway: "HorrorGameMaker.com".

`hound.glb` is used as downloaded (GLB from the sampler's `Models/GLB/hound/hound_flayed.glb`).

`stilts.glb` comes from the rigged .blend: its skin moved from the 431-bone rigify rig onto the plain 21-bone metarig (fingers folded into the hands), materials rebuilt from the jpg maps at 1024 px. It has no clips; the game poses the bones.

`facelings.glb` is the FBX converted to glb with Blender 4.5, every clip kept.
