Converted with ffmpeg: mono, 44.1 kHz, 96 kbit/s mp3, loudness-normalised
to -18 LUFS, leading silence trimmed; the cries are single breaths cut
from the longer recordings, with short fades. The game plays them at varied pitch,
often slowed, through its own positional audio and reverb.
