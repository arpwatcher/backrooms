# 4. Entities: twenty in full, and two more

Every entity has to fit on one card. If its rule takes more than one sentence
to state, the entity is redesigned. Each entry below uses the same fields:

- **Class** - stalker (hunts you), watcher (reacts to being seen), lurer (gets
  you to come to it), hazard (part of the level), or herder (pushes you
  somewhere).
- **Tell** - what reaches you first, always at least 5 seconds before it can
  touch you.
- **Rule** - the one sentence it lives by.
- **Counter** - what works.
- **Co-op** - how it changes with more than one player.
- **Pairs** - its best synergy (§9).

Status: **[built]** is playable now, **[next]** is planned for the next few
sessions, and **[later]** is designed only.

---

### 1. Smiler [built]
- **Class:** stalker. A famous one.
- **Tell:** a grin and two pale points in a dark patch, and a faint wet
  clicking like teeth (every 2.5 s within 14 m).
- **Rule:** it only moves in the dark, and only while you are in the dark too.
- **Counter:** stay under working panels, or hold the flashlight on it - it
  flinches back, and the beam drains battery faster while it does.
- **Co-op:** one player holds the light on it while the others cross the
  dark.
- **Pairs:** Hounds (the light is where you run, and running is loud).

### 2. Hounds [built]
- **Class:** stalker, pack of 2-3. A famous one.
- **Tell:** panting and claws on damp carpet, at least 20 m before they
  reach you.
- **Rule:** they hunt by sound alone.
- **Counter:** freeze or crouch-walk. They sniff the last place they heard
  you for 3 s, then wander off. Don't sprint until you're spent: an exhausted
  player gasps, and they hear that within 5 m even while you stand still.
- **Co-op:** they hear voice chat. A player calling out can save a friend or
  doom them.
- **Pairs:** Smiler, Still Life, and above all the Hushbaby (the counters
  conflict, so the director never pairs those two).

### 3. Still Life [built]
- **Class:** watcher. A homage to the statue that only moves when unobserved.
- **Tell:** stone scraping on carpet for 6 s while it wakes, then a scrape
  every time it has moved and you look back.
- **Rule:** it only moves while nobody is looking at it.
- **Counter:** keep it in view and back away. Watch your lucidity, because low
  lucidity makes you blink.
- **Co-op:** one player watching is enough, so take turns. It is far weaker
  against a group, which makes it a "split up and you're dead" entity.
- **Pairs:** Smiler (both want your eyes), Hounds (back away slowly and
  quietly).

### 4. Deathmoths [built]
- **Class:** stalker, swarm. A famous one.
- **Tell:** a papery fluttering that thickens; moth dust drifts through the
  beam.
- **Rule:** they fly to the brightest light near them.
- **Counter:** turn your flashlight off and stand under a working panel (the
  panel is brighter than you). Moth jelly draws them away.
- **Co-op:** one player can bait them with a light while the others move.
- **Pairs:** Smiler is the hard pair: light keeps the Smiler off and draws
  the moths. It is allowed only on levels with working panels.

### 5. Partygoers [built]
- **Class:** lurer. A famous one.
- **Tell:** distant party music, a balloon drifting at the end of a hall,
  and a "=)" sign taped to a wall.
- **Rule:** they only come for you if you go to the party.
- **Counter:** never walk toward the music. Every room that has a balloon in
  it is theirs.
- **Co-op:** the music only plays for one player at a time, and that player
  hears friends' voices coming from the party room.
- **Pairs:** the Intercom (it gives directions that lead to the party).

### 6. Facelings [built]
- **Class:** watcher. A famous one.
- **Tell:** a person standing too still, at the edge of a room, with no face.
- **Rule:** they do nothing unless you look them in the face for more than
  3 s.
- **Counter:** look away, and pass them with your eyes on the floor.
- **Co-op:** Facelings sometimes copy a teammate's outline, so is that your
  friend?
- **Pairs:** Still Life (one wants your gaze, one punishes it).

### 7. Hushbaby [built]
- **Class:** lurer. An original.
- **Tell:** an infant crying from inside a wall. It starts after 8 s of
  silence within 24 m and swells for 10 s before anything happens.
- **Rule:** it comes when you have been quiet too long.
- **Counter:** make any sound. Walking, or humming (hold V), settles it back
  into the wall.
- **Co-op:** easy with friends who talk, and miserable alone.
- **Conflicts with:** Hounds. The director never pairs them below the noclip
  tier **[built]**.

### 8. Stilts [built]
- **Class:** watcher. A homage to a tall thin figure in a suit.
- **Tell:** ceiling tiles rattling where its head drags along them, and a
  creak of long joints, every few seconds within 26 m.
- **Rule:** it sees anything standing above waist height, and nothing below
  it.
- **Counter:** crouch when its head turns your way. It sweeps slowly, and
  when it does see you it stares for 1.5 s before it strides, which gives you
  time to get down.
- **Co-op:** players can draw its gaze by standing on purpose.
- **Pairs:** Hounds (crouching is quiet, so this is a kind pair), Still Life
  (crouch for one, eyes up for the other), and Deadline (crouching is slow, so
  this is a cruel one).

### 9. The Attendant [built]
- **Class:** hazard. An original.
- **Tell:** a wet mop slapping on carpet within 18 m, the smell of cleaning
  fluid, and your own shoes squelching once they're wet.
- **Rule:** it follows every wet footprint you leave.
- **Counter:** walk around the puddles, or wait for your shoes to dry (8 s
  after the last puddle). With no prints there's no trail. Prints dry out by
  themselves after 40 s.
- **Behaviour:** he mops the trail in order, oldest print first, and when the
  trail ends at you, so does he.
- **Co-op:** shared floor, so one careless player leads him to everyone.
- **Pairs:** Hounds (going around is the long, loud way), Still Life (backing
  away, you can't see where you step).

### 10. Deadline [built]
- **Class:** herder. An original.
- **Tell:** 20 s after you arrive, the lights where you came in go out, then
  the next ring, then the next, with a low climbing tone that quickens as it
  gets close.
- **Rule:** it comes from where you came in, at a steady pace, and never
  stops.
- **Counter:** keep moving toward the exit. At 0.42 cells per second (about
  1 m/s) it is slower than walking.
- **First touch:** pushes it back six cells. It doesn't go home.
- **Co-op:** the slowest player sets everyone's pace.
- **Pairs:** Still Life (look back while you hurry) and Stilts (crouching is
  slow).

### 11. Ushers [later]
- **Class:** herder, a pair. An original.
- **Tell:** two people in uniform, a long way down the hall, pointing.
- **Rule:** they close in on anyone who walks away from where they point.
- **Counter:** go where they point. It is a longer route, but a safe one.
- **Co-op:** they only point for one player. The others are free.
- **Pairs:** Partygoers (sometimes they point at the party).

### 12. Census Taker [later]
- **Class:** stalker. An original.
- **Tell:** a clipboard voice counting out loud, "one... two...", and getting
  the number wrong.
- **Rule:** it hunts whoever is alone when it finishes counting.
- **Counter:** be within 6 m of another player when the count ends. Solo, hide
  in a dead-end and stand still.
- **Co-op:** the whole point of the entity.
- **Pairs:** Still Life (groups are safe from both).

### 13. Intercom [later]
- **Class:** lurer. An original.
- **Tell:** a speaker on the wall crackles, then a calm voice (speech
  synthesis) gives directions.
- **Rule:** one of every three things it says is a lie.
- **Counter:** follow only instructions that a note, a sign or the level
  agrees with.
- **Co-op:** it speaks to each player separately, and the lies differ.
- **Pairs:** Partygoers, Ushers.

### 14. The Guest [later]
- **Class:** hazard (a fate). An original.
- **Tell:** at "gone" lucidity, the room starts to look furnished: chairs, a
  lamp, a welcome mat.
- **Rule:** if you sit down, you stay.
- **Counter:** drink almond water, get under the light, and never interact
  with furniture that wasn't there.
- **Co-op:** others see you sitting and can pull you up (hold E for 3 s).

### 15. Skin-stealer [later]
- **Class:** stalker. A famous one.
- **Tell:** someone who looks like a survivor, walking slightly wrong (the
  knees bend backward for a frame).
- **Rule:** it can only copy someone it has seen alone.
- **Counter:** ask a question only your friend can answer. Solo, it calls you
  by a name you never told it.
- **Co-op:** paranoia is the whole entity.

### 16. Clump [later]
- **Class:** stalker. A famous one.
- **Tell:** wet slapping footsteps, many at once, and several voices
  breathing.
- **Rule:** it is drawn to groups: the more players near each other, the
  faster it moves.
- **Counter:** split up.
- **Pairs:** Census Taker (the cruellest pair: stay together, but don't).
  Allowed only on the highest tier.

### 17. Howler [later]
- **Class:** stalker. A famous one.
- **Tell:** a long howl that echoes, coming from the direction it will come
  from.
- **Rule:** it charges in a straight line at the last place it heard a
  scream (a player's sprint-panic, or a voice above a whisper).
- **Counter:** sidestep out of the line, since it cannot turn mid-charge.
- **Pairs:** Hounds (two different ways to be heard).

### 18. Window [later]
- **Class:** lurer. A famous one.
- **Tell:** a window in an interior wall that should not have one. Behind
  it is daylight, and sometimes home.
- **Rule:** it can only reach you if you stand in front of it.
- **Counter:** don't look out of it, and walk past along the far wall.

### 19. Tannoy [later]
- **Class:** stalker. A homage to a tall figure with a loudspeaker for a head.
- **Tell:** static from a speaker above the walls, then a voice from it
  calling out sounds it has heard (your footsteps and your voice).
- **Rule:** it walks to any sound it can repeat back to you.
- **Counter:** silence, or a wind-up radio to give it something else to
  copy.
- **Pairs:** Hushbaby is a conflict, so the director never pairs them.

### 20. Crawlspace [later]
- **Class:** stalker. A homage to a pale long-limbed thing seen at night.
- **Tell:** ceiling tiles shifting and dust falling a few metres ahead.
- **Rule:** it moves through the ceiling and drops onto anyone standing under
  a missing tile.
- **Counter:** watch the ceiling, and never stand under a dark gap for longer
  than a second.
- **Pairs:** Deadline (you can't stop, but you have to look up).

## Added while building

Two entities came out of play-testing rather than the original twenty. Both
are real 3D models (credits in `assets/models/CREDITS.md`).

### 21. The Wretch [built]
- **Class:** stalker. Our take on the famous Wretches: one of them, alone.
- **Tell:** slow bare footsteps and a wet rasping breath within 18 m, then a
  shriek the moment it commits to a chase.
- **Rule:** it hunts what it can see or hear, and forgets what it can't.
- **Counter:** it runs at 3.5 m/s, slower than a sprint (4.6). Break its
  line of sight, then go quiet in the dark or in a locker: it searches where
  it last saw you for 10 s and gives up.
- **Co-op:** it chases whoever it saw last; a second player can make noise
  to pull it away.
- **Pairs:** Hounds (running from one is how you call the other).

### 22. The Drowned [built]
- **Class:** stalker. An original, for the poolrooms.
- **Tell:** water dripping where nothing should drip, and a slow wet retch.
- **Rule:** he moves only while you move, and as fast as you do.
- **Counter:** stand still and he stops dead. Cross the room in short,
  crouched moves (0.7 m/s for him) and never sprint toward the exit while he
  is close (a sprint brings him at 4 m/s).
- **Co-op:** he answers to the fastest-moving player near him, so one
  careless runner drags him onto everyone.
- **Pairs:** Deadline (it makes you move, and he moves with you).
