# 7. Tasks

Some levels won't let you leave until you've done something. A task always
pushes you out of the lit start into the level. It is introduced by the
level itself (a dead exit sign, a note, a ringing phone), and it gives the
director something to work against: entities are scarier when you have to go
toward them.

Frequency **[built]**: none before level 2, then 30%, rising 4% per level to
a cap of 55%. Like everything else, it comes from the level's seed.

Design rules:

- **Readable from across the room.** A target makes a sound or shows a light
  from at least 10 m away.
- **Every target is reachable.** Targets are placed with the same
  connectivity guarantees as exits, are never on the start or the exit, and
  are spread apart **[built]**.
- **Progress shows in the world.** A thrown breaker turns its corner of the
  level back on **[built]**. A solved task changes the exit visibly.
- **Co-op splits well.** Every task with several targets can be divided
  between players.

## Built

| Task | Tell | Do | Result |
|------|------|----|--------|
| **Restore Power** | the exit sign is dead; a note says there are three breakers | find 3 breaker boxes on walls and throw them (E) | each brings its dead panels back; all three relight the exit |

## Designed [next / later]

**Find and fetch**

2. **The Key**: a key on a lanyard somewhere on the level; the exit is
   locked.
3. **Batteries for the Exit**: the exit is an elevator with a dead panel;
   bring 2 batteries (and decide whether to keep them for your flashlight).
4. **Lost and Found**: return 3 personal items to an empty desk. Each one
   plays a memory when picked up.
5. **Carry the Sleeper**: a body that is breathing, carried to the exit;
   carrying means no running (§6 soaked rules).
6. **Water Run**: fill a bottle at a dripping ceiling tile and bring it to a
   dry plant by the exit. It spills if you run.

**Sound**

7. **Answer the Phones**: a phone rings somewhere. Reach it before the 6th
   ring, three times. Each ring is a homing beacon.
8. **Tune the Hum**: turn three dials until the hum is in tune. Listen to the
   beat frequency, and avoid it near the Hounds.
9. **Find the Music Box**: follow a tune through the level; it gets louder
   when you face it.
10. **Silence the Alarm**: a fire alarm draws everything that hears.
    Find the panel and shut it off fast.
11. **Echo Location**: clap (V) and the exit answers. Only you can hear it.

**Light**

12. **Light the Way**: replace 4 dead tubes along a corridor to open it.
13. **Signal Fire**: carry a firesalt flare to a brazier at the far end.
14. **Dark Room**: develop a photo in a room that must stay dark for 20 s
    (good with a Smiler nearby).
15. **Projector**: run the reel. The film shows the route to the exit.

**Observe**

16. **Census**: count the chairs (or the people) in the level and enter the
    number at the exit keypad.
17. **Witness**: stand and watch a Still Life for 30 s without blinking (a
    lucidity check).
18. **Spot the Difference**: two identical rooms; one changed thing hides the
    key.
19. **Read the Signs**: three signs each show one digit; the code opens the
    exit.
20. **Follow the Arrows**: chalk arrows lead to the exit, and some are lies
    (there is an Intercom on this level).

**Endure**

21. **Hold the Door**: keep a door open for 60 s while the level gets
    louder.
22. **Wait for the Elevator**: the button is pressed; survive 90 s.
23. **Night Shift**: stay in a lit office until the clock hits 5 (3 real
    minutes) while the lights fail one by one.
24. **Hide and Seek**: something counts to 30; then be somewhere it doesn't
    look.
25. **Keep It Lit**: a lamp is running out of oil; refill it twice from cans
    in the level.

**Two-key (co-op)**

26. **Two Switches**: two levers 20 m apart, thrown within 2 s of each
    other.
27. **Pressure Plates**: one player stands on a plate while the other walks
    through the opened wall.
28. **Spotter**: one player watches cameras, the other walks the route.
29. **Relay**: an item must pass through all players' hands before the
    exit.
30. **Hold the Light**: one keeps a beam on a Smiler while the others do the
    task.

**Strange**

31. **Feed the Vending Machine**: it takes three coins and gives a level
    key, or something else.
32. **Sign the Register**: the guest book at the start needs every player's
    name, and someone has already signed yours.
33. **Clock In**: punch a time card. You are late, and something knows it.
34. **Water the Plants**: three plastic plants, watered anyway. They grow.
35. **Ask the Keymaster**: a trade, not a fight. Bring it what it wants.
36. **Return the Balloon**: a Partygoer's balloon, returned to the party,
    without staying.
37. **Do the Dishes**: a sink of dishes in a break room. It takes 40 s and is
    very loud.
38. **Mop Up**: clean the wet trail the Attendant follows.
39. **Stamp the Forms**: five forms in five offices, and the stamp is
    somewhere else.
40. **Leave a Note**: write something for the next person who comes through.
    It shows up in other players' runs (async, opt-in).
