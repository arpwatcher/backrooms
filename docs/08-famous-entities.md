# 8. The famous ones, and homages

Backrooms fans arrive knowing the wiki. Meeting a Smiler should feel like
meeting a celebrity: you know the rule, and the game makes you use it under
pressure. Every famous entity below keeps its folklore rule, so what players
already know is correct. Its threat comes from what the director pairs it
with.

Homages to other horror icons get **original names and their own rules**.
They nod to the source without copying it.

Columns: **Rule** (one sentence), **Tell** (5 s or more before contact),
**Counter**, and **Status**.

## Using the famous ones well

- **Never subvert the famous rule.** A Smiler that suddenly moves in the
  light is a betrayal, not a scare. Use variants (Red Smiler) and clearly
  mark them as different.
- **Let fans feel smart.** The first level where a famous entity debuts can
  be a little easier: the director spends 20% less budget on it.
- **Pair them with the unknown.** The scariest levels mix something you know
  with something you don't, so the known rule helps you and the unknown one
  undoes it.
