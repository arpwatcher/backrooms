# 13. The first ten levels

The first ten levels are composed, not rolled **[built]** in
`src/game/firstLevels.js`. Each layout is still generated from the run's
seed, so no two runs share a map. What is fixed is what kind of place each
level is, its rule shift and its task. Each entity's debut therefore gets
the stage it needs.

The structure is: one new rule per level, introduced alone, in a place that
makes its counter possible. Then a first mix at level 8, and a peak at
level 9 before the checkpoint.

---

### Level 0 - The Lobby
- **Place:** the classic yellow, steady lights.
- **Lives here:** nothing.
- **Teaches:** walking, looking, the red EXIT sign, almond water (Q). Three
  notes on the walls, the first one by the start **[built]**.
- **Feeling:** wrongness without threat. The hum. Things are too quiet.
- **Ends:** the first exit. A fade, and "level 1 - the cubicle maze".

### Level 1 - The Cubicle Maze, Hounds debut
- **Place:** dense partitions, short sightlines.
- **Teaches:** sound. A note says they hunt by hearing and to crouch (C).
- **Moment:** panting two walls away. The player crouches for the first
  time, and the pack runs past the end of the aisle.
- **Why here:** you hear them long before you can see them, which is the
  whole lesson.

### Level 2 - The Dim Wing, Smiler debut, task: Restore Power
- **Place:** a third of the panels dead, flickers.
- **Teaches:** light. The flashlight (F) holds it off, and the battery is
  finite.
- **Task:** three breakers. Each one relights its corner of the floor, so the
  counter to the Smiler is something you earn.
- **Moment:** the grin is visible in a dead patch you have to cross to reach
  the second breaker.

### Level 3 - The Pillar Hall, Still Life debut
- **Place:** one vast room of columns, long sightlines.
- **Teaches:** watching. You hear a scrape for 6 s before it can move.
- **Moment:** you back away from it across the whole hall, and the exit is
  behind you.

### Level 4 - Overtime, Deathmoths debut
- **Place:** after hours, with half the panels off and the rest cold.
- **Teaches:** sometimes light is the danger. Turn off the flashlight and
  move panel to panel.
- **Moment:** the flutter starts the second you switch the beam on in a
  dark stretch.

### Level 5 - The Lobby again, checkpoint, Partygoers debut
- **Place:** back to the classic, as a relief.
- **Teaches:** don't go toward the music. The balloons and the =) sign are
  visible down a hall.
- **Checkpoint:** dying from here on wakes you here.

### Level 6 - The Damp Floor, rule shift: Soaked, Facelings debut
- **Place:** green-grey, rot, wet carpet you can't run on.
- **Teaches:** don't stare. You must walk past them, eyes on the floor,
  since you can't run.

### Level 7 - The Pillar Hall, Stilts debut
- **Place:** open, columns, nowhere to hide but low.
- **Teaches:** crouch when its head turns. It stares for 1.5 s before it
  comes.

### Level 8 - The Cubicle Maze, rule shift: Lights Out Behind You, task: Restore Power
- **The first real mix.** The director picks from everything you've met.
- **Tension:** you have to keep moving, and the breakers are behind you
  somewhere. The lights you relight are the lights you lose.

### Level 9 - The Dim Wing, the peak
- **The director spends 130% of its budget.**
- **The last test** before the checkpoint at level 10. After this the game
  is rolled: archetypes, shifts and tasks are all chosen by seed.

---

## Why these choices

| Level | Debut | The place makes the counter... |
|-------|-------|--------------------------------|
| 1 | Hounds | **necessary**: you can't see far, so you have to listen |
| 2 | Smiler | **earned**: the breakers bring back light |
| 3 | Still Life | **possible**: there is room to back away |
| 4 | Deathmoths | **tense**: the dark is the default, and your light is the risk |
| 5 | Partygoers | **visible**: a calm lobby lets the music and balloons stand out |
| 6 | Facelings | **forced**: no running means you have to walk past them |
| 7 | Stilts | **clear**: open space makes its gaze easy to read |
