# 1. Pitch and pillars

## Pitch

You clipped out of reality and into the Backrooms: endless office space,
humming fluorescent panels and damp carpet that goes on forever. Each level
is generated from a seed. Something lives in each one, and each thing follows
**one rule** that you can learn. Up to four of you go down as deep as you can.
You don't fight anything. You learn, you listen, you hide, and you help each
other through.

The fear comes from not knowing yet, not from being punished. The first time
you meet something you have no idea what it wants. By the third time you know
its rule, and the director has paired it with something that makes that rule
hard to follow.

## Pillars

Every feature is checked against these. If a feature breaks one, the feature
changes, not the pillar.

1. **Every entity is a puzzle with a fair rule.** Each one has a single rule
   that can be stated in one sentence, plus a tell that comes at least five
   seconds before it can hurt you. Nothing kills you without warning. The
   death screen always names what did it and which rule you broke.
2. **Scary, not punishing.** Your first contact with any entity is
   survivable. Checkpoints come every five levels. Low tiers are for people
   who want the atmosphere without the stress, and they are still scary.
3. **The place is the monster too.** The architecture lies. Rule shifts
   change the physics of a level: lights that only work when you can't see
   them, doors that remember which way you came, a level that is slightly
   larger every time you turn around.
4. **Co-op is about trust and information.** Players split up to cover more
   ground, call out what they hear, and carry each other. Some entities can
   only be beaten by two people doing different things at the same time.
5. **Sound first.** You should be able to play with your eyes half closed.
   Every entity has a voice, every surface has a footstep, and the hum is a
   character in its own right.
6. **Seeded, replayable, shareable.** The same seed always gives the same run.
   Players can swap seeds for a good scare.

## What it is not

- It is not a combat game. You will never shoot anything.
- It is not a jumpscare reel. Jumpscares are rare and always earned by a
  long tell before them.
- It is not punishing for its own sake. There are no unwinnable spawns, no
  instant deaths without a tell, and no losing an hour of progress.

## Audience and session length

The game is for horror fans who like learning systems, as in *Lethal Company*,
*Phasmophobia* and *Iron Lung*, and for Backrooms fans who want the
places they know from the wiki to feel real. A level takes 4-12 minutes, a
session is 5-10 levels, and a run to the first checkpoint takes about 30
minutes.
